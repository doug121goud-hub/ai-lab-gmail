Privacy Policy

AI Lab Gmail Connector
Last updated: September 2026

Purpose

AI Lab Gmail Connector is a private application used by its authorised user to integrate Gmail with a personal investment research and reporting system.

Google user data accessed

The application may access Gmail data when authorised by the user. Its Google OAuth permissions may include permission to read Gmail information and permission to send email.

How Google user data is used

Google user data is used solely to provide Gmail functionality requested by the authorised user, including supporting research workflows and sending requested research reports.

Data sharing

Google user data accessed by this application is not sold to third parties and is not used for advertising.

Authentication

Access to Google services is authorised using Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication system. The application does not require the user to provide their Google password to the application.

Data retention

OAuth credentials required to maintain authorised access may be stored securely by the application owner. Gmail data is accessed only as required for the authorised application's functionality and research workflows.

Revoking access

The authorised user may revoke the application's access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings.

Scope

This application is intended for private use by its authorised owner and is not offered as a public Gmail service.
