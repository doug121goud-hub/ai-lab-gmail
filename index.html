AI Lab Gmail Connector

Private Gmail integration for the AI Lab research system.

About

AI Lab Gmail Connector is a private productivity application used by its authorised user to integrate Gmail with a personal investment research and reporting system.

The application can access authorised Gmail functionality to support research workflows, including reading relevant email information and sending research reports requested by the authorised user.

Private application
This application is intended for use only by its authorised owner. It is not offered as a public Gmail service.

Google account access

Google account access is used only to provide the Gmail functionality requested by the authorised user. Access is limited to permissions explicitly granted through Google's OAuth consent process.

Privacy

For information about how Google user data is handled, please read the Privacy Policy.
